# Your ActiveState narrative

This is the language to use when the departure comes up, with recruiters and in
interviews. It is built from your record, not from a template. Three beats:
what happened, where you were standing, what you know now.

## The narrative

"ActiveState restructured around AI. They cut about half the company, roughly
twenty people, across every function. My role was one of them.

I led that transformation. We took the company off its legacy offerings onto an
AI-driven product line built on Claude, and I brought the AI-enabled workflows
into Product, Engineering and go-to-market. It worked. The organization on the
other side was smaller, and that was the point.

Eleven years of platform transitions behind me, and this one happened to be
onto AI. So I can tell you where it genuinely changes how a product
organization plans and ships, and where teams expect more from it than they
get. That is the experience I would bring you."

## Short version, for a recruiter screen

A recruiter screen is six minutes and there is no room to develop the full
answer. Give them this and stop.

"ActiveState restructured around AI and cut about half the company. My role was
part of it. I had been leading that transformation, so I know the change from
the inside."

## When they ask why you

"Half the company, about twenty people, every function. It was not a selection
in the usual sense. The company was rebuilt at a different size."

## When they ask why not here

This is the one that decides the interview. Do not get defensive, and do not
argue that AI is worse than it is.

"It absorbed a real part of it. Leadership looked at what was left and decided
a smaller team could carry it, and I would probably have made the same call.
What I would say to you is that deciding which pieces to hand over is the hard
part, and getting it wrong is expensive in both directions. I have already run
that exercise once."

## On delivery

You can honestly say you led this. That is a real and currently scarce
credential, so lean on it rather than softening it.

Do not learn any of this word for word. Hold the three beats and let the words
come out however they come out that day. Anything delivered verbatim sounds
delivered, and that is the one thing that would undo it.

Do not grade the decision as wrong, even by tone, and skip any joke about it.
Both read as someone still working through it.

Use me as a reference. I will describe it the same way you do: restructuring
around AI, about half the company, every function.

If any of this does not sound like you, tell me and we will change it. It has
to be yours or it will not hold under a follow-up question.
