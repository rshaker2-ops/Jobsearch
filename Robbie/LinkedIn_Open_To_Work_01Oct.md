# LinkedIn, open to work, 1 October 2026

Written 1 October 2026 and handed to Bob to send on. Robbie's to publish or not.

Lives here rather than in `tools/sweep/narratives/` for two reasons. That folder
is interview preparation, and the builder renders whatever it finds there into
the opening section of the person's sweep document, which a published post is
not. The CI check also requires every filename in it to be a profile key, so a
second file for the same person could not go there anyway.

## The post

I'm open to new opportunities in GRC and information security.

The work I do is third-party and vendor risk. I've run 95 vendor risk
assessments and completed 30 customer security questionnaires, and I held 100%
compliance across eight consecutive months. That covers intake and triage,
inherent risk scoring, due diligence review, and the follow-up that actually
closes a finding rather than logging it.

I've also built the automation around that work, including moving a vendor risk
workflow into Vanta and standing up threat intelligence and security news
pipelines.

I work in SOC 2, ISO 27001 and NIST. I'm looking for a GRC, third-party risk, or
security compliance analyst role, remote or in the Boston area.

If your team is hiring, or you know a team that is, I'd welcome an introduction.

## Three things left out on purpose

Do not add these back in a later draft. Each omission was the point.

**No reason for the search.** Not a restructuring, not a role elimination,
nothing. Bob asked for neutral and for no mention of ActiveState. Any stated
reason invites a follow-up in the comments, and on LinkedIn that thread is
public and permanent. The interview answer to this question is a different
document and lives in `tools/sweep/narratives/robbie.md`.

**No tenure.** His written materials are weak exactly here: a reader subtracts
eighteen months and stops before the 95 assessments. A resume needs dates and a
post does not, so this is the one place he can lead with volume and omit
duration without it reading as concealment.

**No adjectives about himself.** No passionate, no detail-oriented. The numbers
are the differentiator against a flood of GRC analysts who describe themselves
instead of their output. Every figure here is from his own record.
